How it works

Three steps. Your agent drives its real logged-in browser; TraceReel does the cinematography.

1

Capture

Your AI agent drives the browser it actually uses — real logged-in state, not a fresh headless session pretending to be you — saving screenshots plus a state/action trace.

tracereel validate trace.json
2

Reconstruct

The engine turns the trace into a full recording: eased cursor glides at 60 fps, click ripples, per-character typing, settled camera shots, crossfades, caption tracks.

tracereel reconstruct trace.json -o demo.mp4
3

Ship

Out comes a polished 1080p60 MP4. Add narration and subtitles in one more command. Reconstructed from real screenshots — never a fake live recording.

tracereel audio demo.mp4 --subtitles both
